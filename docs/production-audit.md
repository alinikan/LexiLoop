## Boundaries still requiring the operator

No user-owned Supabase project, SMTP sender, OpenAI billing key, Cambridge agreement or Vercel/GitHub deployment was created or tested live. Local fixtures do not prove email deliverability, licensed Cambridge rendering for a specific dataset, provider billing/model access, hosted cross-device sessions, backup restoration or physical-iPhone behavior. The README gives exact acceptance steps for these.

The app uses a finite original suggestion catalog plus on-demand generation. Personal sentences are self-assessed after a word-presence check. Completed words persist; unfinished word interactions restart after navigation. Reminders are in-app, with an immediate browser permission test, not scheduled background push. These limits are disclosed in the UI/README rather than represented as implemented infrastructure.
